05 · How it works

Five layers.
One phone call.

1

Ingest every 60 s

LoRa sensor to a clinic gateway; store-and-forward keeps 7 days offline.

ESP32LoRa 865 MHzSQLite
2

Clean door vs drift

Flags door openings, sensor drift and gaps before anything reads the curve.

rulesunit-tested
3

Forecast 45 min ahead

A small model per fridge model, with a calibrated 80% band, not a point.

LightGBMconformal band
4

Decide deterministic

Rules over the forecast choose who to call and when. The model never calls.

rules as configescalation
5

Notify SMS, then voice

The nurse on duty, then the supervisor; every step in the vial's log.

SMSIVRaudit log

Why it holds up

Works offline

The gateway forecasts and calls over a SIM. No Wi-Fi, no cloud on the critical path.

Rules decide, the model advises

A wrong forecast can change a ranking, never skip a call.

Cheap to copy

₹900 per fridge, ₹3,000 per clinic, no per-seat licence.

Measured, not claimed

Every target on slide 08 has a script in the repo.

How we measure it, live

Held out before training: 30 failure days from two fridge models the model never saw. Scored by a script in the repo, run on stage, with the baselines through the same pipeline.

Both baselines are real practice: the fixed alarm most clinics own, and the rate rule a careful engineer would write first.
